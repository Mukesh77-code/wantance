4. The Single Biggest Problem on the Ground

Part B: Vardhan Steelware — Business Requirements Document (BRD)

Phase 1 Order Lifecycle & Shopfloor Tracking System | Strict Constraint: Maximum 4 Pages (~2,100 Words)

1. The Real Story: What’s Going Wrong at Vardhan Steelware

Vardhan Steelware makes stainless steel cookware (Kadais). They press steel in-house, send it to external polishers, and ship to wholesale distributors. Key personnel operate in functional silos:

  • Suresh (Owner): Spends 2 hours daily calling staff to find order statuses.
  • Kunal (Order Coordinator): Manages orders via WhatsApp chats and a standalone Excel sheet.
  • Ramesh (Production/Dispatch Head): Runs stamping presses based on verbal cues and a paper diary.
  • Munshi ji (Accounts): Sits in the office entering invoices into Tally Prime days after dispatch.
  • Outside Polisher: Receives metal in bulk weight (kg) and returns finished utensils in piece counts (pcs).

Consequences: Status blindness, duplicate manufacturing runs, unmonitored polishing shrinkage, and dispatching goods to credit-blocked clients.

2. Real Evidence: The 7 Data Problems Found in Vardhan's Records

5. Functional Requirements (FR)

  • [FR-01] Easy Order Entry & Unique ID Must Have: Kunal enters Customer, SKU, Pcs, Rate, and Delivery Date. System assigns ORD-YYYY-XXXX. Blocks blank customer names and warns on duplicate entries within 48h.
  • [FR-02] Automatic Credit Hold Gate Must Have: Orders for credit-blocked clients move to CREDIT_HOLD and are completely hidden from Ramesh's mobile screen until Munshi ji or Suresh unlocks them.
  • [FR-03] Outsourced Polishing Dual-Unit Tracker Must Have: Captures outward weight (kg) and inward weight (kg) plus pieces (pcs). System calculates: Shortage = Weight Sent - (Weight Returned + Scrap). Alerts if shortage >1.5%.
  • [FR-04] Ramesh’s 3-Tap Mobile Screen (Zero Typing) Must Have: High-contrast mobile view. Ramesh advances stages with 3 taps: [Start Pressing] → [Sent to Polish] → [Ready to Ship].
  • [FR-05] Partial Dispatch Tracking Must Have: Supports multiple tempo shipments per order line, maintaining Remaining = Ordered - Sum(Shipped).
  • [FR-06] Sales Return (RMA) Workflow Should Have: Logs returned pieces, defect reason (Dents, Scratches, Wrong SKU), and disposition (Rework/Scrap). Disallows negative sales rows and alerts Munshi ji for credit notes.
  • [FR-07] Three 8:30 AM Morning Reports Must Have: Automated daily summary of pending orders, blocked orders, and polishing shortages.

6. Non-Functional Rules (Speed & Usability)

• NFR-01 (Mobile Usability): Big touch buttons (≥16px font), readable in dusty shopfloor lighting, works on 5.5-inch Android phones.
• NFR-02 (Speed): Order search and filter return results in <1.0 second on 4G networks across 50,000 records.
• NFR-03 (Role Security): Ramesh sees quantities and sizes, but customer prices and profit margins are completely hidden from the shopfloor view.
• NFR-04 (Audit Trail): Changes to approved quantities or written-off shortages log User ID, Timestamp, Old Value, and New Value.
• NFR-05 (Offline Resilience): Mobile app caches updates during factory Wi-Fi drops and syncs upon reconnection.
